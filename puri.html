Welcome to Annavaram

Annavaram Sri Veera Venkata Satyanarayana Swamy Temple is one of the famous pilgrimage centres of Andhra Pradesh. The temple is situated on the beautiful Ratnagiri Hill.

Lord Satyanarayana Swamy is worshipped here as a form of Lord Vishnu. The temple is especially famous for Sri Satyanarayana Swamy Vratam.

📜 History of Annavaram Temple

Annavaram is a famous holy shrine located in Andhra Pradesh. The temple is situated on Ratnagiri Hill and is surrounded by beautiful natural scenery and the Pampa River.

According to the temple's traditional history, Lord Satyanarayana Swamy appeared in the dreams of a devotee named Earanki Prakasam and Raja I.V. Ramanarayana.

They were guided to the location of the sacred idol on Ratnagiri Hill. The idol was then installed and worshipped in the year 1891.

Initially, a small structure was constructed. Later, the temple developed with the support of devotees and local people.

The temple was reconstructed during 1933–34 and was again renovated during 2011–2012.

🛕 About the Temple

📍 Location

Ratnagiri Hill, Annavaram, Andhra Pradesh.

🙏 Main Deity

Sri Veera Venkata Satyanarayana Swamy.

🏛 Architecture

The temple is built in Dravidian architectural style.

📅 Year

The sacred idol was installed at the present location in 1891.

🙏 Religious Importance

Annavaram is especially famous for the Sri Satyanarayana Swamy Vratam. Devotees perform this vratam seeking peace, prosperity, education and success.

The main deity is described in the temple tradition as a unique manifestation associated with the divine forms of Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva.
