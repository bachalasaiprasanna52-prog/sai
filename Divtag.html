olympics

The Summer Olympic Games, also known as the Summer Olympics
or the Games of the Olympiad, are a major international multi-sport event normally held once every four years.
The inaugural Games took place in 1896 in Athens, then part of the Kingdom of Greece, and the most recent was held in 2024 in Paris, France.
